FROM AN OPEN SLOT TO A CONFIRMED BOOKING

Less back-and-forth.
A clearer calendar.

Your customers choose an available time. OpenBook checks capacity and calendar conflicts, then records the booking.

  1. 01

    A customer chooses a time

    Your booking page shows the service and available slots for your business.

  2. 02

    The booking is confirmed

    New bookings are confirmed immediately. Google Sheets holds the booking and capacity records; Google Calendar receives a confirmed event that blocks the business's time.

  3. 03

    The details arrive by email

    The customer receives an email confirmation with booking details and a management link. Later cancellation and reschedule requests are reviewed by the operator.

FAMILIAR TOOLS, CLEAR RESPONSIBILITIES

Your bookings have
a home.

OpenBook does not centrally host your customer booking data. A deployment runs using Google services and the owner's Google account.

Google Sheets
The authoritative record of bookings and capacity.
Google Calendar
Checked for conflicts and updated with confirmed business events.
Email
Booking details and a management link sent to the customer.
